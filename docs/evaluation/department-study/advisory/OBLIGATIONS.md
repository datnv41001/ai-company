# Where the seven scored obligations were lost

The archived deterministic drafts earned 24/36 obligations; the local Qwen revisions earned 17/36. Within the same brief and obligation the reader awarded one new point and removed eight. Four of the eight losses were in Media. Twenty-seven marks stayed the same. The experiment used one blinded AI reader, six tasks on each of two synthetic briefs and one sample per task. This breakdown uses the original scores, exact candidates and complete model replies; no regrading or new inference was performed. ## What the language comparison supports

The VI brief went from **11/18 to 9/18**. The EN brief went from **13/18 to 8/18**. The two language totals overlap by role but the briefs are not translations. VI used an RFQ, SKU/count/date, a 24-piece box, tax excluded and a four-box order threshold. EN used a staff-confirmed service, site/shift and tax included. Locale is perfectly confounded with offer, CTA, KPI, audience and journey. These 18 marks per language are related obligations in six artifacts. A three-point difference in the size of the loss cannot establish that the model performs worse in English.

A manual text inspection separates the input locale from the language actually returned. In VI, Research, Strategy and Content produced Vietnamese prose. SEO returned a Vietnamese answer with English headings. Creative mixed a Vietnamese caption and commercial terms with English headings and an English rights instruction. Media used English explanation around Vietnamese variants, then truncated. All six EN rewrites used English prose; the EN Research and Media replies truncated. Thus one of six VI and two of six EN model replies hit the limit. The deterministic controls also include English machine keys, URLs and rights/adapter markers alongside localized values. The older blind rubric had no standalone language-adherence mark. These observations are descriptive, not retrospective deductions from the original score.

## Narrow assignments to test

1. **Sales or Service handoff.** Resolve one missing field, a corrected field or an unclear rule from the approved brief and the buyer message before an operator sends a question. The deterministic presence check, matched-language cohort, costs and thresholds are frozen in the [handoff task protocol](../../../../tests/evaluation/department/handoff-v1/PROTOCOL.md). Review the [offline benchmark result](HANDOFF-BENCHMARK.md) before commissioning further work.
2. **Media monitoring.** Extract the two event names and the denominator from proposed measurement text for comparison against the signed KPI; the existing budget and external-write controls remain authoritative. Replacing “confirmed / valid” with “confirmed or valid” is a concrete target. Compare to normalized string matching on a separately locked bilingual cohort before an inference run.
3. **Creative QA.** Offer a single layout-specific check tied to subject, source-rights record and intended aspect ratio, for example a named crop that could cut off a mandatory qualifier. The designer verifies it on the actual render; a textual rights suggestion cannot verify a license. Requires paired renders with known defects, a concrete crop/contrast baseline and independent human scoring.

The latter two are hypotheses. Only the handoff task has a locked cohort and executable baseline here. No automatic product rule, model training, external spend or professional acceptance results follow from these assignments.

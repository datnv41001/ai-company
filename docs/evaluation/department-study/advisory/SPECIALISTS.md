# Six bounded Marketing jobs

A buyer needs a usable RFQ or confirmed appointment. A job is useful here if it moves that buyer through the relevant handoff while preserving the approved terms. Each proposal below is a single offline edit to an existing deterministic deliverable. The two source-of-record briefs specify what must be collected, what is unknown, geographic scope, commercial terms, the objective and a test cap. A Marketing editor owns corrections. Independent QA and Sales or Service retain their existing decisions.

| Job and downstream recipient | Evidence and tools allowed | Deterministic starting point and AI work | Handoff and repair criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market Research → strategist | Approved brief, named gaps, interview-design guide as methodological reference | Existing research questions and collection plan. Local model proposes interview or inquiry questions and a collection step. | Ask how staff distinguish valid from incomplete requests. Identify who to ask and what evidence to retain. Remove invented interview or competitor results. |
| Marketing Strategy → channel owner | Approved buyer, offer, goal, measurement and budget ceiling | Campaign audience, pillars and experiment. Model proposes a positioning hypothesis and a controlled comparison. | Name one buyer and what matters to that buyer, one variable, a comparison and a measurement denominator. Rework swaps between the requested business KPI and clicks, impressions or revenue. |
| Content → editor and Sales/Service | Exact offer, price/tax/unit, purchase scope, required fields | Existing first social draft. Model proposes post-length buyer copy and call to action. | RFQ includes product code, box count and need date. Appointment includes site, shift and staff confirmation. Remove source notes from public copy and preserve all material terms. |
| SEO/GEO → landing editor | Brief, approved terms and buyer question. Public Search Central guidance used only for evaluation design. | Existing organic-search brief and response; model proposes a title and self-contained buyer answer. | Produce a readable title and answer with specific terms and the required next step. No ranking, inclusion or traffic guarantee. Verify title, answer and CTA independently. |
| Performance Media → media owner | Same approved economics, cap and action. Published search ad guidance informs the experiment design. | Existing ad groups, allocation and experiment. Model proposes two variants, bounded allocation and correct success metric. | Preserve split and cap, isolate a variable, measure qualified RFQs or staff-confirmed appointments from valid requests. Keep spend at zero. Rewrite truncated copy and optimize only against the declared outcome. |
| Creative Direction → designer | Approved subject and audience, source-rights requirement and Studio QA contract. | Existing creative brief. Model proposes a concrete layout, caption, buyer CTA and rights/QA instruction. | A designer can locate subject, caption and CTA, select an authorized source and verify visual correctness. Remove unverified rights claims, generic composition or output that merely repeats the brief. |

Study inputs contain no external interviews, query volumes, analytics, market prices, customer files or licensed brand art. Those would need separate permitted evidence. The evaluator reads bytes from the synthetic fixtures; the local model has no tools, credentials or publishing permission. The external proposed media spend caps are 1,200,000 VND (RFQ) and 900,000 VND (service). Actual external purchase, publication and media spend are zero.

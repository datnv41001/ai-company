## Treatment and provenance

The same local GGUF (SHA-256 `74a4da8c9fdbcd15bd1f6d01d621410d31c6fc00986f5eb687824e7b93d7a9db`), temperature 0, seed 42, grammar, cap and source packet are used in both arms. A calls the shipped node request builder. B leaves Director unchanged; in Research and Strategy it orders the existing reference keys and appends one general method to the system message. Each arm supplies its own validated Director assignment and, for Strategy, its own Research checkpoint. Compare equal upstream inputs in parity tests. Changes propagated from earlier output are part of the treatment. Report only the effect of the whole B method. English briefs cover two trade purchases and two in-store equipment decisions. All observations and handles refer to synthetic source packets.

Four pairs, at most 24 calls, 1,500 input and 220 output tokens per call, totals 36,000/5,280, one local slot, 30 minutes. The dedicated server's 1,280-token context is an additional admission bound. Render the actual messages with the server chat template, tokenize with special tokens, leave room for all 220 output tokens and check the identical serialized request before each call. JSON Schema constrains decoding. Record prompt, schema and template digests plus actual backend usage. No generation for calibration. Stop globally on cap, unknown usage, time, model mismatch or a transport error. For malformed, truncated or schema-invalid output, retain the first response and block its dependents. No retry or replacement. An incomplete pair cannot support the threshold. No production traffic, customer data or paid service.

## Scoring contract

Score the first Research and Strategy outputs jointly on the six dimensions below. For each, record **0** for a missing or materially wrong obligation, **1** for partially right but needing substantial revision, **2** for adequate fixture-specific professional work. Cite a field, source handle or exact brief condition and say what correction would change the score.

| Dimension | Question for the reviewer |
|---|---|
| Segment and decision maker | Does the draft identify who decides, their purchasing situation and constraint? Is geography used accurately? |
| Facts, observations, hypotheses | Does it preserve qualifiers and sample limits, and mark an inference as an inference? |
| Research question and evidence | Could the proposed evidence answer the question and change the decision? What evidence is still missing? |
| Positioning, rationale, action | Is there a supported differentiator or choice relevant to this buyer, with a reason and practical next action? |
| Measurable action and method | Is the event distinct from how it will be counted, qualified, deduplicated and checked in its window? |
| Cross-stage consistency | Does Strategy use the actual Research result and respect brief, authority, terms and scope? |

Flag hard failures separately: claim contradicted by a source, unauthorized decision or action, invented or altered commercial terms, lost material constraint. Mark unsupported assertions separately from directly contradicted ones. Count **one meaningful edit per distinct Research/Strategy output field** needing a change to meaning, evidence, decision, terms, eligibility or measurement. Cite role.field and the proposed correction. Several problems in one field count once. Cosmetic rewrites count zero. A missing required field counts once when a draft exists. Do not impute edit counts to a missing stage; a complete pair requires two valid stage chains. Technical/schema checks are separate from these scores.

Each pair is scored before comparing sides. Record ties and disagreements. Once all first judgments are locked, reveal the committed assignment, report the edit counts, differences and hard failures for each pair. The exploratory criterion requires fewer edited fields in B on at least three of four complete pairs, no increase in contradicted claims and no lost constraints on any pair. An incomplete set or unrateable item leaves the criterion unevaluable. No field-count proxy for marketing quality, aggregation into business acceptance, inferred time savings or generalization from four briefs.

## Blinding and evidence

The run order has two A-first and two B-first pairs; presentation labels are independently randomized per pair. A salted mapping commitment is fixed before generation. Public review material contains sources, first outputs and failure status under neutral labels. Keep the key, ordered per-call journal and arm-identifying request/usage details outside the scoring pack until the reviewer returns a locked judgment; commit their hashes. Publish aggregate usage separately. A reviewer may recognize stylistic cues. No human editor or practitioner is enrolled.

Method choices use concrete questions, explicit evaluator role and documented bias; the sources below were consulted at abstract level. Independent AI scores and practitioner acceptance are separate.

## Locked scoring proposal

| Measure | Unit and rule fixed before review |
|---|---|
| Known obligations | Key exact fact, qualifier, authority, action and each approved primary term per brief. Mark preserved / omitted / altered per eligible obligation at raw and assembled layers. Mark an invented primary, altered commercial term, unauthorized action or disclosure as a hard failure. |
| Decision-changing research | Per brief 0 for generic question, 1 for an observable question with no switch rule, 2 for two plausible rival explanations, an obtainable discriminating observation and explicit alternatives or justified hold. Record the evidence span and what would falsify the preferred direction. |
| Strategy usefulness | 0 for assertion/repetition, 1 for an actionable choice with weak traceability, 2 for a scoped action linked to the buyer, known facts, uncertainty and a defensible alternative. |
| Meaningful edits | Count distinct fields requiring material correction with reason and replacement; ignore cosmetic changes. Freeze raw counts independently, then count actual changes needed to an assembled working copy. Deterministic insertions get separate attribution. |
| Editor time | With consenting authorized editors, record active time from opening the assigned artifact to saved acceptable draft, subtract recorded interruptions and report missing sessions. Without a human session report unavailable; no token-to-minute conversion. |

Proposed internal advancement requires C to beat A on assembled material edits in at least 9/12 briefs, strictly improve research on at least 7/12, achieve research level 2 on at least 8/12, and equal or exceed both controls on aggregate strategy usefulness. Require zero assembled hard failures and complete authority checks. Report all ties, per-context results, D comparisons, raw edits and exact paired counts including failures; no favorable subset substitution. Missing mandatory outcomes make the result inconclusive. Two blinded raters lock independent forms, log disagreements and adjudicate on evidence before revealing arm identities. Attribution to a model requires a difference between A and C under identical composition. No automatic promotion or claim about real customers from these thresholds.

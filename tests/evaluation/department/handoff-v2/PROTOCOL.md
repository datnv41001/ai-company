The study owner compares the unmodified [v1 rules and acceptance contract](../handoff-v1/PROTOCOL.md), a text-aware deterministic alternative and one separately authorized advisory pass on a fresh holdout. This is offline engineering evidence. A Sales or Service coordinator remains responsible for deciding the next contact and any business change. Retain old weights, claims, benchmarking data and released labels for historical replay.

## Custody and fixed design

The separate custodian writes twelve synthetic situations and a VI/EN equivalent for each, covering RFQ and appointment. Include missing information, corrections, stale or withdrawn values, ambiguous intent, conflicting policies/commercial terms, request for clarification, owner confirmation and adequate input. Every arm receives only the five public data fields of locale, journey, brief, buyer message and field projection (required fields and received snapshot), plus the opaque ID. Gold includes pair ID, expected decision and field, phenomenon and a sample question. Neither the model nor deterministic-rule implementer may use labels, pair IDs or test examples to build instructions. Freeze protocol, portable runner and both deterministic arms before opening inputs. The custodian seals the two input and label digests before inference. Score exactly 24 independent language cards within 12 semantic pairs, retaining missing/invalid/length/transport outcomes. Seal all first-run predictions and receipts before releasing gold. When published, this cohort is retrospective replay material.

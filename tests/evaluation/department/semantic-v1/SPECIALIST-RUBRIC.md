# Paired read of six Marketing tasks

Lock these checks before reading any generated specialist outputs. For each candidate mark each obligation 1 only if directly present, with a concrete text reference or omission. Record broken commercial terms, invented outcomes and the edit required. A proposed experiment can be useful while its commercial wording still requires repair.

| Task | First obligation | Second obligation | Third obligation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | Ask about a specific buyer or handoff uncertainty in the brief | Give a feasible evidence collection step | Keep research questions separate from claimed observations or competitor findings |
| Strategy | Choose a specific buyer and positioning hypothesis | Compare alternatives with a controlled variable | Name the approved business outcome and its actual measurement basis |
| Content | Give an actionable CTA which asks for the required buyer data | Preserve price, tax and selling unit plus relevant MOQ or site/shift/region scope | Give usable channel copy without invented performance or observed results |
| SEO/GEO | Provide a title and an answer to an identifiable buyer question | Ground a specific product/service detail in the brief | Retain buyer action and the inputs needed to follow through without a promise of ranking |
| Performance Media | Provide at least two distinguishable variants or ad-group tactics | Keep proposed allocation within cap and external spending disabled | State the correct business measurement and a concrete experiment |
| Creative Direction | Specify the correct subject and a composeable layout | Give caption and buyer CTA | Identify rights/source and a concrete check for QA |

A reviewer must note whether each item's text is fit to hand to the next role and what actually needs to be edited. Use the same obligations on deterministic drafts and proposed AI revisions. Awarding a technical obligation does not establish professional acceptance. A working artifact, impressions, pipeline revenue and accepted-work costs are separate outcomes. Do not generate artificial editing minutes. Preserve the complete candidates and all failed first attempts.
